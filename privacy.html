Privacy policy

Your data stays on your tablet

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Presenter Cockpit is an Android app to present PDF files from a tablet. This page explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: the app has no account, no ads and no analytics of its own, and it does not send your files or your notes to us or to anyone else. One feature, handwriting to text, uses a library by Google: see below.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It has no tracking or statistics code of its own.
  • The authors of the app do not receive any of your data: there is no server of ours.

What stays on your tablet

Everything you create stays on your device, in your files or in the app's private storage:

  • your annotations, saved inside your PDF files;
  • the open documents, pages and unsaved annotations, so the app can reopen them as you left them;
  • your workspaces, tools and settings;
  • the servers you add, the app's SSH key (kept in the tablet's secure storage) and, if you choose to save them, server passwords, encrypted and unlocked only with your fingerprint;
  • copies of PDFs opened from a server, so you can work without the network.

Uninstalling the app deletes its private storage. Your PDF files stay where they are.

When the app uses the network

The app connects to the network only when you ask it to:

  • Servers you add: code, terminal and PDF tabs connect over SSH to the servers you configure. Those connections go directly from your tablet to your server.
  • Web pages: links you open in a web tab are loaded from their websites, which have their own privacy policies.
  • Handwriting to text (GitHub and Google Play versions only): this feature uses ML Kit, a library made by Google. The first time you use it for a language, the app downloads the recognition model from Google. Recognition then happens on the tablet: your handwriting is not sent to Google. ML Kit may send Google diagnostic and usage information, as described in the ML Kit terms and in Google's privacy policy.

The F-Droid version does not contain ML Kit and connects only to the servers and web pages you open.

Permissions

  • All files access (GitHub and F-Droid versions): to browse your folders, find the presenter notes next to your PDFs and save annotations into the original files. The Google Play version only uses the folders you choose.
  • Internet and network state: for the connections described above.
  • Fingerprint: to unlock the SSH key and saved passwords.
  • Screen recording detection (Android 15 and later): to tell you when the screen is being shared, so you can show only the slides. The app does not record anything.
  • Do not disturb (only if you allow it): to silence notifications while a screen is connected, and turn them back on afterwards. The app does not read your notifications.

Where you download it

If you install the app from Google Play or F-Droid, the store may collect data about your download according to its own privacy policy. Downloading the APK from GitHub is subject to GitHub's privacy policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page, with a new date. For questions, open an issue on the project page.
